Frequently asked questions

Clear answers for a project still taking shape.

Is the coin live?

The JCQ token mint and distribution setup are pending. Juicy Journey mission recognition works independently and does not require a live token.

What is the token name, ticker, and mint address?

Juicy Couture Coin, intended ticker $JCQ, on Solana. The mint address is pending deployment. Always verify the full mint address.

How does Juicy Journey recognition work?

Complete one of six useful missions across Care, Help, and Build. An authorized reviewer verifies the result. Earn one non-cash Spark stamp per distinct mission; Spark, Charm, and Crown recognize breadth of contribution. Attendance, tips, and token holdings earn no automatic stamps.

Can I buy the coin from this website?

Official buy links arrive after launch verification. The wallet section supports separately reviewed transfers and supported swaps; connecting never sends funds.

Is this affiliated with the fashion label?

No. This independent project is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Juicy Couture or its owners.

One useful outcome. One Spark stamp.

Choose a mission, describe the result, and ask an authorized reviewer to check it against the published criteria. Each mission can earn one stamp per wallet, ever. Repeating the same activity does not add stamps.
